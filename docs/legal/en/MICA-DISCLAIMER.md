# MiCA disclaimer – Fallcoin (FLC) and yFLC

> **DRAFT – not legal advice, must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer (CZ/EU; US only if needed).**

1. **Testnet only.** FLC and yFLC exist only on the **Stellar testnet**. Testnet assets have no monetary value, can be
   reset at any time, and cannot be bought, sold or redeemed for money.
2. **Not an offer.** Nothing in this repository, app or website is an offer to the public, a solicitation, a white paper
   under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA), investment advice or a financial promotion. The FLC reference value of
   0.33 USD is a **display/testing parameter**, not a price, a peg or a promise of value.
3. **yFLC is frozen.** No issuance, distribution or transfer is planned.
4. **No crypto-asset service.** The project does not provide custody, exchange or transfer services for clients. The
   wallet is non-custodial: keys stay on the user's device (Secure Enclave / Android Keystore) and are never exported.
5. **Off-ramp.** Card or bank cash-out is performed only by independent licensed providers (active: MoonPay, Coinbase;
   allow-listed, not active: Anycoin). Users contract directly with them, under their terms, KYC/AML and licenses.
6. **Before any mainnet launch:** a MiCA classification (ART/EMT/other crypto-asset), a white paper and its notification,
   possibly CASP authorisation (ČNB), AML/CFT (Czech Act No. 253/2008 Coll.), GDPR, consumer protection
   (14-day withdrawal right) and tax analysis are required. See `docs/mica-checklist.md`.
7. **US persons:** no availability is intended. US securities and commodities law analysis only if needed.
