# US civil complaint – structural outline only (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT – NOT LEGAL ADVICE. Not notarized. Not for filing as-is.** Generic public-law outline for orientation only; must be reviewed and adapted by a licensed US attorney in the relevant jurisdiction. Placeholders in `[BRACKETS]` must never be filled with secrets, bank data or personal identifiers in this repository.

**This is a table of contents, not a pleading.** A complaint must be drafted and signed by a licensed attorney (Fed. R. Civ. P. 11).

1. Caption – `[COURT]`, `[PLAINTIFF]` v. `[DEFENDANT]`, `Case No. [assigned by clerk]`
2. Nature of the action – one paragraph
3. Parties – `[PLAINTIFF – description, no ID numbers here]`, `[DEFENDANT]`
4. Jurisdiction and venue – §1331/§1338, personal jurisdiction facts, §1400(a)
5. Facts – the work, authorship, publication, registration `[REG. NO. if any]`, access, copying, notice history
6. Claim(s) for relief – e.g. Count I: Copyright infringement (17 U.S.C. § 501)
7. Prayer for relief – injunction, damages, fees, other relief
8. Jury demand (Fed. R. Civ. P. 38) – optional
9. Signature block – attorney only
10. Exhibits – index of evidence (kept outside this repo)
